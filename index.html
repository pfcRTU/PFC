About Us

PFC – Photography and Filming Club is the official visual arts club of Rajasthan Technical University. We're a vibrant community of passionate photographers and filmmakers who love capturing the world through our lenses.

Dedicated to creativity and innovation, we explore new techniques, share knowledge, and produce stunning visual content together. Whether you're a beginner or a pro, PFC offers a platform to learn, collaborate, and showcase your talent.
